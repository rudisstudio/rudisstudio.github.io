Let's Smash privacy policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Let's Smash ("the game") is made by Rudis Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Let's Smash on Android.

Your use of the game is also covered by the Rudis Studio terms of use.

In short

We do not run any servers, the game needs no account, and we do not sell any personal information. The game shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one, and has no analytics or tracking of its own. You can buy coins and bundles with real money; Google Play handles the payment, and the in-app purchase software from Unity that is built into the game sends Unity a record of each purchase, as described below. Apart from purchases and ads, the game plays fully offline.

Information we collect

The game does not send us your name, email address, location, contacts, usage statistics or crash reports, and we cannot see how you play. We receive information about you only in two cases:

  • When you buy something: Google Play gives us the order records we need for refunds and our accounts, such as the order number, the item, the price and the country of purchase. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • When you email us: see "Contacting support" below.

What Unity receives when you buy something is described under "In-app purchases".

Information kept on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Let's Smash saves some data in the game's private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: the level you are on, your coins, your lives with the time your next life is due, your rocket uses, and any unlimited-lives time you have left;
  • a record of the purchases the game has already given you (the purchase's transaction number and how many times each item was bought), so that each purchase is given to you exactly once;
  • your settings: music, sound and vibration on or off;
  • whether you last chose the rocket booster before a level, and which new pieces and tips the game has already shown you.

The saved progress carries a signature so the game can tell if it was changed outside the game. This data is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it. Your phone's own system backup may include this data if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by us.

In-app purchases

You can play the whole game without paying. If you choose to, you can buy coin packs and bundles inside the game. Purchases are optional and need an internet connection.

Google Play handles every purchase: you pay through your Google account, with the payment method you have set up in Google Play. The game and we never see your card or bank details. Google handles this information under Google's privacy policy, and your purchases appear in your Google Play order history.

Unity: the game uses Unity In-App Purchasing, software from Unity Technologies, to talk to Google Play. When you make a purchase, this software sends Unity a record of it, encrypted in transit:

  • the item bought and the purchase's transaction number;
  • Google Play's receipt for the purchase;
  • an installation identifier that Unity creates for the game on your phone, and a session identifier;
  • technical details of your device and its operating system, the game's version and when it was installed;
  • your consent settings (the game does not grant Unity advertising or analytics consent, so these are not granted).

Unity's servers may also estimate your approximate location (your country) from your IP address. Unity uses this information to run its in-app purchase service and for analytics about it. Unity sends these records whenever a purchase is made; there is no setting in the game to turn them off, so if you do not want them sent, please do not make purchases. They do not include your name, your email address, your payment details or your advertising ID. We do not receive this information from Unity. Unity handles it under the Unity privacy policy.

Ads

Let's Smash shows only rewarded ads, and only when you ask for one: to get an extra life when you have run out, or an extra use of the rocket booster. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. You can play the whole game without watching any ads, and players who have bought anything in the game are not offered ads.

The ads are served by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. When the app starts and has an internet connection, this SDK connects to Google and prepares an ad, even if you never watch one. To do this, Google collects and shares the following information automatically, encrypted in transit:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your device's general location (not your precise location);
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics about ad delivery, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google handles it under Google's privacy policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: before any ad is loaded, Google's consent form asks whether Google may use your information for personalised ads. You can choose not to consent. Google may then still show non-personalised ads, which use less information (for example your general location from the IP address), and it still uses some information to measure ads and prevent fraud. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy options.

Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (depending on the phone, under Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads).

Contacting support

The Support button in the game's Settings opens your own email app with a new message to us. Before you send it, the message already contains your phone's model, its Android version, the game's version and your phone's language, so we can look into problems; you can read or remove these lines before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

The Terms and Privacy buttons open our terms of use and this policy in your web browser. The Privacy options button, shown only where the consent form applies, opens Google's consent form again.

Permissions

Let's Smash asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration: gives short buzzes when the cannon fires, when a rocket or TNT crate goes off, and when you win. The vibration effects run on your phone and send nothing. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used for in-app purchases and ads, to reach Google Play and Google's ad servers and to check whether the phone is online.
  • Google Play billing: lets the game offer purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: lets Google's ad software read the Android advertising ID, as described under Ads.
  • A few system permissions added by the ad software for its own work, such as Android's ad services (Privacy Sandbox) and keeping short background tasks running.

The game does not use your precise location, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your files.

Third parties

Besides the purchase software and Google's ad software described above, Let's Smash contains no third-party analytics or tracking code. It is built with the Unity game engine, made by Unity Technologies. When the game starts with an internet connection, the engine may send Unity a small set of technical information, such as the device model, operating system, screen and graphics hardware, and the engine version. This does not include your name or your progress in the game, and we do not receive it. Unity handles it under the Unity privacy policy.

When you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of providing the store, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Let's Smash is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed for children on Google Play. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved on your phone. We delete your support emails on request. We keep Google Play's order records only as long as we need them for refunds and our accounts, or as the law requires.

The purchase records described under "In-app purchases" are held by Unity, not by us; the game cannot delete them. To ask Unity about them, or to ask for their deletion, follow the instructions in the Unity privacy policy. If you need help, email us and we will help you make the request. Your Google Play purchase history is managed in your Google account.

Information Google collects for ads is held by Google; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage it under Google's privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding crash reporting, analytics or another ad partner, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
