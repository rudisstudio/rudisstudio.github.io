Let's Smash privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Let's Smash ("the game") is made by Rudis Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Let's Smash on Android.

Your use of the game is also covered by the Rudis Studio terms of use.

In short

We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information, and the game needs no account. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking of its own. The game plays fully offline.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information, and we cannot see how you play.

Information kept on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Let's Smash saves some data in the game's private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: the level you are on, your coins and your lives, with the time your next life is due;
  • your settings: music, sound and vibration on or off;
  • whether you last chose the rocket booster before a level, and which new pieces the game has already introduced to you.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it. Your phone's own system backup may include this data if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by us.

Contacting support

The Support button in the game's Settings opens your own email app with a new message to us. Before you send it, the message already contains your phone's model, its Android version, the game's version and your phone's language, so we can look into problems; you can read or remove these lines before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use what you send only to answer you, and you can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

The Terms button opens our terms of use in your web browser.

Permissions

Let's Smash asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration: gives short buzzes when the cannon fires, when a rocket or TNT crate goes off, and when you win. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
  • Internet: included by the game engine the game is built with (Unity). The game itself sends nothing over the internet; see "Third parties" below for what the engine may send.

The game does not use your location, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your files.

Third parties

Let's Smash contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code of ours. It is built with the Unity game engine, made by Unity Technologies. When the game starts with an internet connection, the engine may send Unity a small set of technical information, such as the device model, operating system, screen and graphics hardware, and the engine version. This does not include your name or your progress in the game, and we do not receive it. Unity handles it under the Unity privacy policy.

When you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of providing the store, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

Let's Smash is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed for children on Google Play. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete, apart from any support emails you have sent us, which we delete on request. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved on your phone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding crash reporting, analytics, ads or purchases, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
