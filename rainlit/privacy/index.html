Rainlit privacy policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

Rainlit ("the app") is made by Rudis Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Rainlit on Android.

In short

Rainlit does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It cannot connect to the internet: the app does not request Android's internet permission.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information, and we cannot see how you use the app.

Information kept on your device

To remember your choices, Rainlit saves a few settings in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • the soundscape you last listened to;
  • for each soundscape, the preset, the volume of each sound layer and how often effects such as thunder play;
  • your settings for 3D sound, the rain animation and lightning flashes.

These settings never leave your device and are not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes them. Your phone's own system backup may include them if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by us.

Permissions

Rainlit asks Android for these permissions:

  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps the rain playing, and shows the media notification, when the screen is off or you leave the app.
  • Wake lock: stops the phone from pausing the sound while it plays in the background.
  • Network state: included by the audio playback library Rainlit uses (Google's Media3). It only lets the library check whether the phone is online. Rainlit has no internet permission, so nothing can be sent anywhere.

To turn 3D sound on and off automatically, the app checks whether headphones are connected. This happens on your device and needs no special permission. Nothing about your headphones is stored or shared.

Third parties

Rainlit contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. When you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of providing the store, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

Rainlit is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding purchases through Google Play, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
