Sudoku Fest privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Sudoku Fest ("the app") is made by Rudis Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Sudoku Fest on Android.

In short

Sudoku Fest does not collect, share or sell any personal information, and it sends nothing anywhere. It works fully offline and needs no account. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information, and we cannot see how you use the app.

Information kept on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Sudoku Fest saves some data in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your settings, such as the theme, highlights, mistake limit and vibration;
  • your progress: the next level and wins for each format and difficulty, and which daily challenges you have solved;
  • your statistics: games played and won, best scores and times, and streaks;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it. You can also reset your statistics inside the app. Your phone's own system backup may include this data if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by us.

Permissions

Sudoku Fest asks Android for one permission:

  • Vibration: gives a short buzz when you make a mistake. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.

The app does not use the internet, your location, your camera, your microphone or your files.

Third parties

Sudoku Fest contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. When you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect information as part of providing the store, under Google's privacy policy. We do not receive personal information about you from Google.

Children

Sudoku Fest does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding ads, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
