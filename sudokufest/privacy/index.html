Sudoku Fest privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Sudoku Fest ("the app") is made by Rudis Studio ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Sudoku Fest on Android and on the web.

In short

We do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information, and the app needs no account. It has no analytics and no tracking of its own. The app offers optional rewarded ads, which you only see when you choose to watch one. Those ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google's ad software in the app collects some device information to show ads, as described below.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, usage statistics, crash reports or any other information, and we cannot see how you use the app.

Information kept on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Sudoku Fest saves some data in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • your settings, such as the theme, highlights, mistake limit and vibration;
  • your progress: the next level and wins for each format and difficulty, and which daily challenges you have solved;
  • your statistics: games played and won, best scores and times, and streaks;
  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes it. You can also reset your statistics inside the app. Your phone's own system backup may include this data if you have Android backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account's settings, not by us.

Ads

Sudoku Fest shows only rewarded ads, and only when you ask for one: to get an extra hint, or to take a Second Chance after three mistakes. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. You can play the whole game without watching any ads.

The ads are served by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. When the app starts and has an internet connection, this SDK connects to Google and prepares an ad, even if you never watch one. To do this, Google collects and shares the following information automatically, encrypted in transit:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your device's general location (not your precise location);
  • app interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics about ad delivery, and to prevent fraud and abuse. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google handles it under Google's privacy policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

We may later add AppLovin MAX as a second ad partner; if we do, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Your choices about ads

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland: before any ad is loaded, Google's consent form asks whether Google may use your information for personalised ads. You can choose not to consent. Google may then still show non-personalised ads, which use less information (for example your general location from the IP address), and it still uses some information to measure ads and prevent fraud. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices.

Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (depending on the phone, under Settings > Privacy > Ads or Settings > Google > Ads). The game also plays fully offline; only ads need an internet connection.

Permissions

Sudoku Fest asks Android for these permissions:

  • Vibration: gives a short buzz when you make a mistake. You can turn it off in the app's Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only by the ad software, to load ads and check whether the phone is online.
  • Advertising ID: lets Google's ad software read the Android advertising ID, as described above.
  • A few system permissions added by the ad software for its own work, such as Android's ad services (Privacy Sandbox) and keeping short background tasks running.

The app does not use your precise location, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your files.

Web version

You can also play Sudoku Fest in a web browser at rudisstudio.github.io/sudokufest/play/. The web version collects nothing and shows no ads.

Your progress, settings and statistics are kept in your browser's storage for that site, on your own device, and are not sent to us or anyone else. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives visitors' IP addresses when they load the page; GitHub handles this under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

The sections "Ads", "Your choices about ads" and "Permissions" above apply to the Android app only.

Children

Sudoku Fest is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and is not listed for children on Google Play. If you believe a child under 13 is using the app, please contact us.

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved on your phone. For information Google collected through ads, use the ad settings described above, or see Google's privacy policy for how to make a request to Google.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by adding a new ad partner, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
